A pretend bakery, a very real build. Maison Pulci exists to prove the point: hand
this starter genuine content — a menu, a story, opening hours — and it stays a
near-monochrome, dark-first site where one violet→rose gradient does the talking.
No client framework. No spinners. Croissants load faster than they bake.
