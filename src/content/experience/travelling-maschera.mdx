Before the web, the mask. Four hundred years in the Commedia dell'Arte travelling
circuit taught me that the best way to land a point is to wear a face that lets
you make it. The bit changes; the discipline doesn't.

## What travels well

- **Conviction in the choice.** A mask works because it commits. The same is
  true of a starter template: pick the opinions, defend them, ship them.
- **Timing.** "Loads instantly" is a punchline. Setup is everything.
- **Reading the room.** Different stages, same audience: people who'd rather
  be doing something else. Don't waste their time.
